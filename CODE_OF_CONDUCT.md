# Code of Conduct

tix-mobile is a small project with a public issue tracker. This page says what is expected of everyone who takes
part in it, in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Expected

- Be specific and be kind. Criticise code and decisions, not people.
- Assume the other person has a reason, and ask for it before arguing with it.
- Accept a no on a feature or a design without reopening it under a new title. Disagreeing once, clearly, is fine.
- Respect people's time: search before filing, include what was asked for in the template, and say when something
  is resolved.
- Write for people whose first language is not English. Plain words over idioms.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual language and imagery, in any form.
- Personal attacks, including about someone's skill, background, identity or employer.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a person or a topic after being asked to stop.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would consider unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Mail <hi@tix.red>. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report concerns the maintainer,
GitHub's [abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
reaches GitHub instead.

Include what happened, where (links are best), and when. You do not need to have been the target to report it.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens, in proportion to what was done: a private note, a public correction, removal
of the content, a temporary ban from the project's spaces, or a permanent one. Whoever is affected by a decision
hears the reason for it.

This applies equally to maintainers and contributors.
